About NVibe AI

Commercial Intelligence.
Delivered where deals are won.

MedTech commercial teams sit on years of pricing history, GPO contracts, and competitive signals — but none of it is connected at the moment deals are made. NVibe closes that gap, delivering the right intelligence inside Salesforce exactly when your rep needs it.

Why NVibe Exists

MedTech commercial complexity is unlike anything horizontal AI is built to handle.

In 2023, Nirav founded NVibe AI because generic AI falls short in MedTech's structured, regulated, and contract-driven commercial environment. The problem isn't that AI can't write an email or summarize a document. The problem is that the data that would actually change deal outcomes exists — it's just inaccessible at the point of decision.

GPO Pricing Floors

Reps negotiate without knowing their own GPO floor price. NVibe surfaces your contracted minimums alongside historical deal patterns — before they enter the room.

IDN Contract Intelligence

IDN terms are buried in legal folders nobody opens at deal time. NVibe reads your contracts and delivers the relevant terms inside Salesforce when they matter.

Competitive Displacement Windows

Competitor recalls, contract expirations, and formulary changes create 30–90 day windows. NVibe monitors signals across 12+ data sources and flags them in real time.

Margin Performance

Discount variance across your field sales team compounds quietly. NVibe shows what comparable deals actually closed at — and the probability cost of going deeper.
